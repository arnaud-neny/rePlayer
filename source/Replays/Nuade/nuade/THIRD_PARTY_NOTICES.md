## Eagleplayer data

These files hold data copied unchanged from the eagleplayers that UADE ships. They are not covered by nuade's MIT
license; the eagleplayers state no license.

- `players/pokeynoise/pn_waves.c`: the waveforms of POKEYNoise V0.25alpha by Achim Härtel.
- `players/davidhanney/dh_data.c`: the waves and tables of the David Hanney player module V1.1 ((c) 1992 David
  Hanney, adapted by Mr.Larmer/Wanted Team & DEFECT).
- `players/majortom/mt_data.c`: the noise waveform and period table of the Major-Tom-Player-Module 1.1 (Major Tom of
  Classified, adapted by marley/INFECT & Eagleeye/DEFECT).
